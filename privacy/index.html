Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

JobaJoba is an app connecting people in Bhutan who need a task done with people willing to do it. This is the same text shown in the app.

1. What we collect

Your phone number, name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash). A profile photo if you add one. The tasks, offers, messages, photos, voice messages, ratings and reports you create, any location you choose to share in a chat, and who you block. If you verify your identity: a photo of a document that shows your face, and a selfie. If the app crashes: a crash report with your device model and system version.

2. How we use it

To run JobaJoba: signing you in, showing tasks, connecting posters with workers, confirming who you are, calculating ratings, keeping the community safe, and fixing crashes. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Location

Your phone's location is used on your phone to work out how far tasks are and to suggest your dzongkhag. When you sort tasks by nearest or filter them by distance, your position is sent with that search so we can find tasks near you; it is not saved. A task you post is stored with its area (a neighbourhood or dzongkhag, not an exact spot) and the description of the place you type in. If you choose to share your location in a chat, its exact position is stored with that message, shown only to the other person in the chat, and deleted with the chat. Photos have their hidden location data removed before they are uploaded.

4. Who can see what

Other users can see your name, photo, verification badge, rating and reviews. Your phone number, email and verification documents are never shown to other users. Messages are seen only by the two people in the conversation — except that when someone reports a problem on a task, our moderators can read the messages on that task to deal with the report.

5. Your verification documents

Your document photo and selfie are kept in private storage and seen only by our reviewers. They are deleted 30 days after we approve or reject them, and immediately if you delete your account. The copies on your phone are deleted as soon as they are uploaded.

6. How long we keep things

Chats, with their messages, photos, voice messages and shared locations, are deleted about 3 weeks after their task is closed. If someone has reported a problem on that task, the chat is kept until the report has been dealt with. Messages you unsend are kept, visible only to our moderators, until the chat is deleted. Declined offers are deleted after 30 days. Everything else is kept while your account exists.

7. Services we use

Supabase stores our database, files and sign-in, on servers in Mumbai, India. Sentry receives crash reports. Google Maps draws the maps, so it receives the location of tasks you look at (only the rough area, until you are matched on a task), your internet address and basic device information. Supabase and Sentry process data only to provide their service to us. Google also uses information from the map, such as device details, crash reports and how the map is moved, to run and improve its own services, under Google's privacy policy.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time from Settings. We delete your name, phone number, email, profile and task photos and verification documents, and cancel your open tasks and offers. Messages, chat photos and voice messages you sent, and reviews you left, stay visible to the other person, shown as "deleted user", because they are their record too. Chats are still deleted about 3 weeks after their task closes.

9. Who JobaJoba is for

JobaJoba is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you think we have, tell us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.

11. Contact

To ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted: jobajobaapp@gmail.com.
