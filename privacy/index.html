Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

JobaJoba is an app connecting people in Bhutan who need a task done with people willing to do it. This is the same text shown in the app.

1. What we collect

Your phone number, name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash). A profile photo if you add one. The tasks, offers, messages, photos, videos, voice messages, documents, ratings and reports you create, any location you choose to share in a chat, who you block, and which chats you mute and which notifications you choose. If you verify your identity: a photo of a document that shows your face, a selfie, and a scrambled code for the phone you send them from. If the app crashes: a crash report with your device model and system version. If you turn on notifications: a code that identifies your phone to Apple's and Google's notification services, so we can send notifications to it. If you add them: an About me, the kinds of work you do with your years and a line about each, what you have (such as a car or your own tools), the languages you speak, photos of your work and photos of certificates. Your Free now posts and trips, the carry requests and invitations to help you send or receive, the steps you mark on a task (On my way, Arrived, Started), the tags you pick when you rate someone, and your task alert settings.

2. How we use it

To run JobaJoba: signing you in, showing tasks, connecting posters with workers, confirming who you are, calculating ratings, keeping the community safe, and fixing crashes. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Location

Your phone's location is used on your phone to work out how far tasks are and to suggest your dzongkhag. When you sort tasks by nearest or filter them by distance, your position is sent with that search so we can find tasks near you; it is not saved. A task you post is stored with its area (a neighbourhood or dzongkhag, not an exact spot) and the description of the place you type in. If you choose to share your location in a chat, its exact position is stored with that message, shown only to the other person in the chat, and deleted with the chat. Photos have their hidden location data removed before they are uploaded. Free now posts and trips show only the dzongkhag and the area you type, never where you are, and a carry request is stored with its pickup area, like a task. If you turn on task alerts, we save your position rounded to about a kilometre so we can tell you about tasks near you, and update it as you move; it is deleted when you turn alerts off or delete your account.

4. Who can see what

Other users can see your name, photo, verification badge, rating and reviews. They can also see your About me, skills, what you have and languages, a “Checked” mark on a skill whose certificate we approved, your work photos once we have approved them, and how many people chose each “what went well” tag for you. The “what went wrong” tags are seen only by our moderators, and your earnings only by you. Signed-in users can see your open Free now posts and trips, unless one of you has blocked the other; a carry request is seen only by the sender and the traveller. Your phone number, email and verification documents are never shown to other users. Messages are seen only by the two people in the conversation — except that when someone reports someone on a task, our moderators can read the conversation between those two people to deal with the report. People you have a task conversation with can see whether you are online, or when you were last in the app; nobody else can, and blocking someone hides it from them.

5. Your verification documents

Your document photo and selfie are kept in private storage and seen only by our reviewers. They are deleted as soon as we have approved or rejected them, and immediately if you delete your account. The copies on your phone are deleted as soon as they are uploaded. When we approve your document, we keep a scrambled code made from its number, never the number itself, so the same document cannot verify a second account. We also keep a scrambled code for the phone you sent it from, so our reviewers can see if other accounts were verified on the same phone. Both are deleted if you delete your account. Certificate photos for a skill are handled the same way: kept in private storage, seen only by our reviewers, and deleted as soon as we have checked them.

6. How long we keep things

Chats, with their messages, photos, videos, voice messages, documents and shared locations, are deleted about 3 weeks after their task is closed. If someone has reported a problem on that task, the chat is kept until the report has been dealt with. Messages you unsend are kept, visible only to our moderators, until the chat is deleted. Declined offers are deleted after 30 days. Everything else is kept while your account exists.

7. Services we use

Supabase stores our database, files and sign-in, on servers in Mumbai, India. Sentry receives crash reports. Google Maps draws the maps, so it receives the location of tasks you look at (only the rough area, until you are matched on a task), your internet address and basic device information. Supabase and Sentry process data only to provide their service to us. Google also uses information from the map, such as device details, crash reports and how the map is moved, to run and improve its own services, under Google's privacy policy. Notifications you turn on are delivered by Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and, on iPhones, Apple's notification service; they receive the code that identifies your phone and what the notification says. When you send a link, your phone opens the page to make its preview (title, description and picture), so that website sees your internet address, as when you visit it. The preview is sent with your message: the other person's phone doesn't contact the website.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time from Settings. We delete your name, phone number, email, profile and task photos, verification documents, skills, About me, work photos, certificates and task alert settings, close your Free now posts and trips, and cancel your open tasks and offers. Messages, chat photos, videos and voice messages you sent, and reviews you left, stay visible to the other person, shown as "deleted user", because they are their record too. Chats are still deleted about 3 weeks after their task closes.

9. Who JobaJoba is for

JobaJoba is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you think we have, tell us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.

11. Contact

To ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted: jobajobaapp@gmail.com.
